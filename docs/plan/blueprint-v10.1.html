Observe, understand, believe, value, allocate, act, remember

Algorik observes the world through information rather than prices alone, models what causes what, remembers what it has seen, holds beliefs with stated confidence, values assets that have no continuous price, allocates with quantum optimisation, acts in microseconds through regional nodes, and learns from every path it declined.

The cognitive loop

Seven stages. Every outcome re-enters at three points.

1 · observe
Ingestion

Prices, books, news, filings, releases, chain events, registries, physical indices, and the platform's own fills and verdicts. Everything resolved to entities.

2 · understand
World & causality

Events update the entity graph. Causal edges are re-estimated. New relationships enter as hypotheses with a falsifier attached.

3 · remember
Episodic memory

State compressed to a vector, stored with what followed, indexed so a similar situation can be retrieved later.

4 · believe
Belief state

Evidence combined into explicit distributions with confidence that propagates directly into position size.

5 · value
Valuation

Assets without a price are marked: curves, credit, surfaces, comparables, discounted cashflows, commitments. Each mark carries its method.

6 · allocate
Capital & quantum

Split across families, regimes and horizons under the risk envelope, with an explicit share reserved for information gain.

7 · act
Regional execution

Nodes combine shipped intelligence with live local state, check feasibility, gate, and execute in microseconds.

8 · learn
Counterfactuals

Outcomes and paths declined are both scored. The self-model updates. Exploration redirects toward where confidence is weakest.

Cognition

Every other plane reasons about instruments, prices and capital. This one reasons about the world that produces them, and about the platform itself. Four capabilities separate reaction from cognition: model the world causally, remember episodically, reason counterfactually, and know what you do not know.

A statistical model predicts. A causal model explains. Only the second survives a regime it has never seen.

Resolution confidence is recorded per link, and low-confidence links are excluded from sizing.

Causal inference

The largest gap in version 8. Everything there was association. When a regime broke, models that had learned the same spurious structure broke together, and nothing could say which relationships should have survived.

How edges are established

  • Natural experimentsreleases, expiries, rebalances, forks
  • Instrumental variableswhere a valid one exists
  • Structural constraintsarbitrage and accounting identities
  • Own order flowthe cleanest evidence available
  • Hypothesis + falsificationthe only source of new edges

Own order flow deserves emphasis. It is the one intervention the platform controls, with a known cause and a measurable effect. Every fill is a small experiment.

What it is used for

  • Regime-break survivabilityreduce before degradation, not after
  • Shock propagationthrough mechanism, not correlation
  • Hidden concentrationpositions sharing a driver
  • Feature validationpredictive with no causal path is capped
  • Explanationa path through the graph, not a weight

Honest limits

LimitHandling
Causal discovery from observational data is unreliableEdges carry confidence. Low-confidence edges inform exploration, not sizing
Markets are non-stationaryEdges are conditioned on regime and re-estimated. An edge that fails its conditions is retired, not patched

Episodic memory

Streaming estimators forget by construction. An exponentially weighted covariance is a moving average; a reservoir is a thin cross-section. Neither answers the question a human trader answers instinctively — have I seen this before, and what happened?

What an episode holds

  • State vectorcompressed market and world state
  • Regimethe label in force
  • Causal contextwhich edges were active
  • Beliefswhat was believed, with confidence
  • Actionswhat was done, and declined
  • Outcomewhat followed, over several horizons
  • Surprisehow far outcome was from expectation

When one is stored

  • Every material actionirreplaceable
  • Every high-surprise momentmost informative, rarest
  • Every regime transitionwhere models fail
  • Periodic in calmso normality is represented
  • Every veto and near-missas informative as action

Episodes are compressed meaning, not retained observation. Years of them at meaningful density is single-digit gigabytes, comfortably inside the data policy.

Retrieval is approximate nearest neighbour over a few hundred dimensions across a few million episodes — milliseconds on a warm-path service. Regions receive a compact digest of the outcome distribution for the current neighbourhood, not the index itself.

Belief state

Version 8 produced point estimates. A slippage number, a regime label, a pressure score. Nothing could distinguish a confident estimate from a coin flip, which meant both were sized identically.

Belief stateSizing response
High confidence, strong edgeFull size within the grant
High confidence, weak edgeSmall size, taken because it is reliable
Low confidence, strong edgeReduced size, and flagged to the exploration budget as worth learning about
Low confidence, weak edgeNot taken
Conflicting evidenceDistribution widens. Size scales down with its width; the conflict is stored as an episode
No evidenceDistinguished explicitly from conflict. Prior only, minimum size or abstain

Distinguishing an absence of evidence from a conflict of evidence is one of the sharpest practical differences between a cognitive system and a statistical one. Both look like a middling score to a point estimator, and they call for opposite responses — abstain in the first case, investigate in the second.

Beliefs are formed centrally, where the evidence is, and shipped as priors with a TTL. A region never forms a belief — it has no world model to do so. Past the TTL, sizing falls back to a fixed conservative multiplier and the region reports operating without current belief.

Counterfactual learning

The cheapest large improvement available. The gate already logs every veto including silences; nothing scored them. If four hundred cycles are vetoed in a day and three hundred and eighty would have been profitable, that is an enormous signal discarded daily.

What gets shadow-executed

  • Vetoed by the risk gateprotective, or merely expensive?
  • Filtered by profitabilityis the cost model too pessimistic?
  • Rejected by feasibilitywhich thresholds cost opportunity?
  • Not whitelistedwas the allocation right?
  • Alternative pathis routing choosing well?
  • Alternative sizingwhere is sizing systematically wrong?

Why it is nearly free

Event-anchored snapshots already capture book state at every veto. That was designed for execution-quality analysis and turns out to be exactly what counterfactual reconstruction needs.

  • Reconstructfrom existing snapshots
  • Simulatevenue's observed fill behaviour
  • Chargerealistic fee, slippage, impact
  • Attributeto the rule that declined it
  • Runtimebatch, preemptible, spare capacity

A control may only be loosened through the full approval path, never automatically from counterfactual evidence. A rule that looks expensive may be the only thing standing between the platform and a tail event.

The exploration budget

A declared share of deployed capital allocated to information gain rather than expected return. A line item in the capital engine, not a side effect.

  • SelectionUCB or Thompson over uncertainty
  • Boundsevery probe has a maximum loss
  • Accountingseparate from return-seeking capital
  • Measureduncertainty resolved per unit spent

Reporting exploration separately matters more than it looks. Mixed into performance it reads as drag and gets cut. Separated, its return is measurable in the improvement of the estimates it bought.

Source discovery

Ingestion consumes known sources. A discovery crawler finds ones it does not yet know about, across three tiers of the web — surface, deep and, under strict controls, dark. It does not pull content in. It registers useful, lawful, recurring sources and creates feeds; everything then flows through the ordinary pipeline.

The platform ends up with a catalogue of sources and a set of feeds, not a copy of the web. Pass-through applies without exception.

The dark web, and the hard line

Monitoring the dark web is legitimate and defensive — learning that the platform's own credentials leaked, or a venue it uses was breached. There is a bright, legal line.

  • Permittedown credentials, venue breaches, threat indicators, already-public sentiment
  • Excluded, hardany material non-public information
  • Excluded, hardstolen data, leaked documents, illicit sale
  • Enforcedat classification, before registration

Trading on non-public information is insider trading regardless of source. The line is legal, not technical, and is built so crossing it is not possible through the layer. The crawler runs in an isolated enclave with no path to any capital-moving component, and every crawl, rejection and registration is audited.

The deep web — where the edge actually lives

The deep web is not the dark web. It is the unindexed majority of the internet: content behind query forms, dynamic rendering, free registrations and licensed subscriptions. Regulatory databases, customs records, court dockets, procurement portals, shipping trackers, job boards, governance forums, pricing and inventory pages. All of it lawful and public, almost none of it read by the people who move markets. A hedge fund cannot deploy meaningful size on a customs filing. How it feeds training

Deep-web facts enter training exactly as any other fact does — through the world model, causal graph, beliefs and episodes, never as raw content.

  • Customs filingWorldEvent → causal graph: supply drives revenue
  • Hiring surgeentity attribute → regime and event models
  • Procurement awardWorldEvent → belief for that entity
  • Governance proposalresolution source and date → calibration
  • Port congestionphysical index → carry and basis models
  • Product price changemarketplace observation → product arbitrage

Freshness is the field that matters most, and it is measured, not assumed: the platform records how far each source's facts preceded the same fact appearing elsewhere, and ranks sources on it.

Why this is the right kind of edge

  • Lawfulpublic, licensed, or a legitimate registration
  • Durabledoes not decay under competition like latency
  • Small-capital compatibletoo small for institutions to bother
  • Compoundingevery source improves the world model
  • Explainablea customs filing has a plain-language story

Governance: every source carries a terms-of-use status — public, licensed or restricted — and restricted sources are not registered. Politeness is enforced (robots directives, rate limits, back-off, an identified user agent). Licensed sources stay within licence with per-source credentials. The dark-web exclusions apply unchanged: material non-public information is excluded wherever it was found. Registration is not ingestion.

The distinction between deep and dark is one of access, not legality. The deep web is crawled broadly and used fully, within terms. The dark web is crawled narrowly and used defensively. Both feed the same pipeline, and the same exclusions sit at the front of it.

Corporate actions looks like plumbing and is not. Without it an equity position quietly becomes wrong after the first split, cost basis drifts, reconciliation halts, and every downstream number is contaminated. It is a prerequisite, not an enhancement.

Every mark carries its method

A mark without a method is an assertion. Every valuation records how it was arrived at, so a portfolio holding a quoted equity and a venture position is not summing two numbers that mean different things without saying so.

Marks decay. A last-round valuation six months old carries less confidence than one six days old, and confidence enters sizing and the risk envelope — so an uncertain mark cannot silently support leverage. Past its staleness limit, a position is excluded from collateral and leverage until refreshed.
