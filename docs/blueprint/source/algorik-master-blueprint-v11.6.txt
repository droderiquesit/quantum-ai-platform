1. Executive Summary
Algorik is not a trading bot and not merely an investment platform. The intended system is a global cognitive
financial operating system that continuously senses the world, builds and updates a causal world model,
generates hypotheses, trains specialist models, allocates capital, manages assets and liabilities, creates and
trades markets, executes across regions, learns from every action and non-action, and maintains one
authoritative multi-asset ledger of truth. Its intelligence is designed to be open-ended rather than fixed: the
platform can discover new information domains, extend its ontology and memory, create specialist brains,
acquire new tools, generate its own research curriculum, evolve models, learn from regional reflex experience
and promote measured improvements through a controlled evaluation pipeline. Its learning system is explicitly
dual-stream: world data teaches the platform why conditions and prices may change, while tick/order-book data
teaches it how liquidity, price formation, venue behavior and market reactions actually unfold. Those streams
are fused into one opportunity and decision model. The cognitive architecture is explicitly multi-model: it
maintains numerous concurrent world models at different domains, regions, horizons and hypothesis branches
rather than forcing all reality into one monolithic representation. A neuro-symbolic reasoning fabric connects
neural perception and generative models to causal graphs, rules, temporal logic, constraints, probabilistic
reasoning and executable proofs. An ambient model mesh runs continuously in the background, proactively
forecasting, searching for weak signals, detecting contradictions, generating scenarios and waking specialist
brains before a human or strategy asks a question. It is also an agentic causal system: beyond forecasting what
may happen, it can reason about which authorized actions could change the probability of a desired outcome,
simulate those interventions, execute them through governed tools, and learn their measured causal effect.
The architecture has two fundamental nervous systems. The slow cognitive system searches, reads, verifies,
models, simulates, reasons and trains. The fast reflex system receives compressed knowledge, models, policy and
capital envelopes, then reacts to local market state in microseconds to milliseconds without waiting for the
global brain. Between them sits a warm coordination layer for cross-region arbitrage, capital movement,
portfolio management, market creation and settlement. Inside those nervous systems are two complementary
learning fabrics: World Intelligence and Market Microstructure Intelligence. Raw ticks are not poured
indiscriminately into the largest AGI model; they are normalized, reconstructed, replayed and converted into
learned microstructure representations that can be fused with world beliefs and distilled into reflex models. A
third connective capability - the Causal Agency & Intervention Engine - turns beliefs and goals into bounded
action plans across communications, capital, products, operations, research and permitted market actions, then
feeds observed effects back into the world-model federation.
The major correction to v10.1 is architectural rather than cosmetic: intelligence is no longer "global and runs
once," regional nodes are no longer isolated except for policy and outcomes, and quantum is no longer restricted
to allocation. Instead, intelligence is a federation: a global cognitive core plus specialist brains plus regional
reflex intelligence. Regional nodes communicate through a low-latency Reflex Mesh, enabling coordinated 2-20-
leg opportunities while preserving local execution autonomy. Quantum is a hybrid research/optimization
accelerator used where measurable advantage exists, including portfolio and path optimization, graph
problems, feature/ensemble selection, selected quantum machine-learning experiments and simulation
research. v11.6 further makes cognition polycentric: world-model populations may disagree and branch,
symbolic reasoners preserve explicit invariants and proof traces, and proactive ambient models continuously
maintain forecasts and surprise scores. Quantum resources can assist selected combinatorial search,
scenario/ensemble selection and experimental reasoning workloads, but never confer truth or bypass evidence
and deterministic controls.

1.2 Non-Negotiable Safety/Correctness Boundary

1.3 External World Data Is Pass-Through; Knowledge Is Durable
Algorik must not become a warehouse of the world's raw external content. Web pages, filings, news,
social/public discussion, product pages, regulatory records, geospatial feeds and other third-party world sources
are viewed and processed as transient inputs. The system extracts evidence and knowledge, records how to find
the source again, then discards the external content.
   Persist the source reference, provider/source identity, retrieval timestamp, version/etag when available,
    cryptographic hash/fingerprint, entitlement, geography, parser/version lineage and freshness/expiry
    metadata.
   Persist compact knowledge: normalized facts, entities, relationships, events, causal edges,
    embeddings/features, summaries, beliefs, confidence, contradictions, novelty, timestamps, world-model
    deltas and memory episodes.
   Historical reasoning re-fetches the original source or licensed historical API using its manifest. If the original
    content cannot be retrieved, Algorik still retains the derived knowledge plus the fact that the evidence is no
    longer re-fetchable.

AI and AGI can originate hypotheses, models, strategies, forecasts, explanations, opportunity intents and capital
recommendations. They may also act automatically inside a pre-authorized operating envelope. However,
money movement, leverage, exposure, settlement and venue access remain bounded by deterministic, testable
controls. This preserves the ability to make the intelligence more general without making the blast radius
general. Market-data training must preserve event time, sequence, venue identity and entitlement lineage and
must prevent look-ahead leakage, timestamp contamination and impossible fills in replay. Ambient models may
proactively originate research, warnings, opportunity candidates, simulations and proposed actions, but their
continuous operation does not increase their authority: symbolic verification, evidence policy, risk envelopes
and deterministic money-moving controls still define what can execute. External intervention is held to the same
standard: truthfulness, provenance, identity, disclosure, market-conduct rules, channel permissions and
jurisdiction are checked before action. The platform does not use fabricated identities, fake consensus, deceptive
content, undisclosed promotion, wash activity, spoofing, rumor engineering or other manipulative conduct to
manufacture an outcome.

6. Global Scout & Data Acquisition Fabric
The Scout Fabric is a continuously operating discovery system. It does not wait for a human to add a feed. It
searches for new lawful sources, measures whether they add unique predictive information, creates or proposes
adapters, monitors access/terms, and retires low-value sources. The goal is not to hoard the internet; it is to turn
the changing world into structured evidence with provenance.

6.1 Source Universe
   Exchange feeds, order books, trades, quotes, options surfaces, funding, borrow, rates and venue status. Raw
    market events feed both the live reflex path and the historical Market Intelligence & Tick Learning Fabric,
    subject to venue entitlements and retention rules.
   Filings, earnings, regulatory portals, court/docket data, procurement, patents, corporate registries,
    government statistics and central-bank releases.
   News, press releases, expert publications, public web, deep-web databases, public social signals and public

8. Market Intelligence & Tick Learning Fabric
World data teaches Algorik why markets may move. Tick and order-book data teaches it how prices, liquidity,
queues, spreads, venue behavior and cross-asset reactions actually evolve. Both are required. The tick fabric is a
first-class learning system, not merely a feed handler for execution.
The design deliberately separates raw market-event storage from the Financial AGI context window. Every raw
tick can remain available for exact replay and retraining, while the cognitive layer consumes time-aligned
market states, learned embeddings, microstructure regimes, causal reaction summaries and exception episodes.
This preserves information without forcing a generalist model to ingest billions of low-value events directly.

Replay is the bridge between historical market truth and safe autonomous learning. A strategy is evaluated
against the market that existed, not against bars created after the fact. The simulator must model what Algorik
could have known and done at each instant, including data arrival, decision latency, queue position, rate limits,
fees, rejects, partial fills, impact and the possibility that a multi-leg opportunity disappears before completion.

8.4 World + Market Intelligence Fusion
The two learning streams meet through a time-aligned fusion layer. World Intelligence supplies events, entities,
causal hypotheses, beliefs, uncertainty and expected propagation paths. Market Intelligence supplies observed
reaction, microstructure regime, liquidity, flow, lead/lag structure, pricing dislocation and execution feasibility.
The fused representation lets Algorik learn not only that an event occurred, but how information is being
transmitted through markets and whether a tradable reaction still exists.
   World event -> expected impacted entities/assets/venues -> watch graph and hypothesis priors.
   Tick reaction -> measured propagation, liquidity response, cross-asset timing and opportunity decay -> belief
    update.
   Mismatch between world belief and market behavior -> investigation, arbitrage/relative-value hypothesis or
    evidence-quality challenge.
   Fused state -> strategy/model foundry, Asset/Capital/Risk brains and specialist brains.
   Distilled fused state -> regional reflex models and compact opportunity maps for millisecond/microsecond
    decisions.

8.5 Data Correctness and Training Guardrails
   No look-ahead: training/replay may use only data that would have been observable by the decision
    timestamp, including realistic publication/feed arrival delays.
   No impossible fills: a replayed trade must respect available depth, queue/priority assumptions, rate limits,
    latency, partial fills, venue rules and price impact.
   No timestamp laundering: source time, receive time, normalized time and uncertainty remain distinct; clock
    corrections are lineage-tracked.
   No hidden survivorship: delisted instruments, venue outages, symbol changes, corporate actions and failed
    products remain represented in historical universes.
   Entitlements are part of lineage: model artifacts record which licensed market data trained them and where
    that artifact is permitted to run/use outputs.
   Raw tick volume is not intelligence by itself: models and the Evaluation Brain must prove incremental
    predictive/execution value versus simpler baselines.

9.5 Proactive Ambient Model Mesh

cash, positions, inventory, obligations, fees, tax lots, collateral, margin, commitments, transfers and settlement
state. Market data can disappear and models can be retrained; the ledger cannot be reconstructed from guesses.
      Global journal with idempotent event IDs and immutable lineage from intent -> order -> fill -> settlement ->
       position/cash impact.
      Subledgers for trading, treasury, custody, physical inventory/commerce, fees/taxes, private commitments
       and user/mandate ownership.
      Continuous external reconciliation with venues, banks, custodians, wallets, warehouses/marketplaces and
       settlement agents.
      Every model/strategy/capital/risk decision stores its version identifiers for complete explainability and
       replay.


16. Regional Reflex Nodes
A Reflex Node is the fast nervous system for one execution region. It does not wait for the global brain. It runs a
signed package of models, strategies, beliefs, feasibility rules, capital grants, risk limits, target inventory and
opportunity definitions, and combines that package with live local state.
Each Reflex Node embeds a non-blocking Rust fabric client and local append-only event journal. The decision
thread never waits on the fabric: it writes to preallocated lock-free queues/journals and continues. Durable
outcomes, telemetry and policy/model acknowledgments are drained asynchronously to the regional fabric.
Cross-region arbitrage coordination that is truly latency-sensitive remains direct peer-to-peer Reflex Mesh traffic
rather than broker-mediated messaging.

17. Global Reflex Mesh & 3-20-Leg Arbitrage
The Reflex Mesh and the Event & Control Fabric are deliberately separate. Reflex Mesh messages are short-lived,
latency-sensitive peer state used to coordinate live multi-leg opportunities. Fabric messages are
durable/replayable control, journal and outcome records. A fabric outage must not stall a live local decision, and
a Reflex Mesh outage must not prevent durable journaling or control distribution.
The earlier "no cross-region call" rule is too absolute for the product vision. The corrected rule is: no remote
dependency is required for a local hot-path decision, but reflex nodes may exchange latency-sensitive state and
coordinate distributed opportunities. Cross-region physics cannot be removed, so the architecture uses pre-
positioned capital/inventory, synchronized opportunity epochs, bounded TTLs and saga-style recovery rather
than pretending distant legs are atomic.

17.1 Arbitrage Graph
Represent every tradable conversion as a directed edge. A node is (asset, venue, representation, settlement stage,
region). Edges include price/rate, fees, size-dependent slippage, carry/funding, borrow cost,
transfer/bridge/logistics cost, tax/duty estimate, settlement risk, probability of fill and time. Cycles of 2-20 legs are
evaluated for executable expected value, not theoretical spread.

24. Causal Agency & Intervention Intelligence
Prediction is incomplete agency. A cognitive financial system should also understand which variables it can
legitimately influence, which actions are available to it, how those actions propagate through the world, and
whether the observed outcome was actually caused by the intervention rather than merely correlated with it.
The Causal Agency & Intervention Engine therefore sits between cognition and tool execution. It turns desired
outcomes into testable, policy-constrained causal action plans and turns action results back into learned causal
structure.

24.1 Desired Outcomes, Utility and Constraints
A desired outcome is represented as a typed GoalSpec rather than a free-form instruction. It declares the target
state/distribution, entities affected, time horizon, success metric, acceptable uncertainty, budget, risk envelope,
legal/jurisdictional boundary, identity that may act, prohibited side effects and stop/rollback conditions. Goals

The system separates outcome preference from action permission. Wanting an outcome never implies
permission to use every causal lever that might achieve it.

24.2 Action-Affordance & Causal Lever Graph
The World Model Federation is extended with an action-affordance graph: which variables Algorik can observe,
which it can control, which tools can affect them, expected latency/cost, causal paths to outcomes, known side
effects, dependencies and authority requirements. Symbolic reasoning enforces hard constraints while learned
causal models estimate uncertain effect sizes.
Interventions are represented explicitly using structural causal models and counterfactual branches where
practical: do(action) -> intermediate states -> target outcome. When causal identification is weak, the engine
abstains, gathers more evidence or proposes an experiment rather than pretending correlation is control.

24.3 Intervention Planner
The planner generates multiple candidate action sequences, including a no-action baseline. It simulates each
across competing world models, market/tick digital twins and scenario branches, then compares expected causal
effect, confidence, cost, capital usage, time-to-effect, reversibility, legal eligibility, conduct risk and downside.
Candidate interventions can be parallel, sequential, conditional or adaptive. A plan may say: gather evidence
first; if condition A is confirmed, publish an attributable research note through an authorized channel; if
market/liquidity state B then hedge or provide liquidity; if response C occurs, stop or switch strategy.

24.4 Authorized Action Surfaces
Algorik acts only through registered tools with explicit identities, permissions and audit. Example action
surfaces include:
   Truthful publishing and communications - attributable articles, research notes, disclosures, reports or posts
    through authorized channels with source lineage, factuality checks, disclosure policy and immutable audit.
   Authorized stakeholder outreach - messages, requests for information, customer/vendor/partner
    engagement or negotiation through approved identities and channels.
   Research actions - acquire lawful data, run surveys/experiments where permitted, request additional
    evidence, launch simulations or commission a specialist research campaign.
   Product and pricing actions - adjust Algorik-controlled product configuration, pricing, availability or service
    behavior where governance permits.
   Operational actions - routing, logistics, inventory, compute, deployment, scheduling, sourcing and other
    owned-system changes.
   Capital and risk actions - allocate, hedge, rebalance, reserve liquidity, move collateral or reduce exposure
    within signed authority envelopes.
   Market/product actions - quote, provide liquidity, create/list/seed permitted markets or instruments, or
    execute commerce/trading actions subject to venue, jurisdiction and market-conduct controls.

24.6 Experimentation and Causal Effect Attribution
After an intervention, the Evaluation Brain estimates what changed because of the action. Where feasible this

and explicit no-action baselines. The platform records uncertainty and never upgrades correlation into causal
certainty without evidence.
Effect is measured at multiple horizons. A communication may change knowledge immediately, behavior later
and market/operational outcomes still later. The attribution object preserves the causal chain, confounders,
competing explanations and confidence rather than recording only a final KPI.

24.7 Ambient Agency Loop
Ambient models can proactively surface a DesiredOutcome candidate or ActionOpportunity when they detect a
risk, opportunity, information gap or controllable causal lever. That does not directly cause action. The Attention
Router sends it to the Agency Brain, which builds a GoalSpec and intervention candidates, runs simulation and
policy/conduct gates, and obtains whatever authority the action class requires.
The recursive loop is: Observe -> predict -> specify desired state -> identify controllable levers -> generate
interventions -> simulate against many worlds -> verify authority/conduct -> act -> observe -> attribute causal
effect -> update world models, action policies and future intervention priors.

24.8 What the System Learns About Agency
The platform maintains an Action Outcome Memory containing intervention context, audience/environment
state, action sequence, causal hypothesis, predicted effect, actual observations, attribution confidence, side
effects and no-action counterfactual. Over time it learns which actions work, for whom, under which regimes,
with what delay, and when not acting is the superior intervention.


25. Governance, Legal Isolation & Authority
A full-capability system must be able to connect to many regulated domains without assuming one legal entity is

an opportunity that the execution layer is prohibited from acting on. The same separation applies to causal

   Jurisdiction/product eligibility gate before strategy deployment and before every new venue/instrument

   Separate identities and credentials for trading, custody/transfer, market creation, banking/financing and
    commerce purchasing.
   Human-signed or governance-signed authority envelopes for destinations, leverage, product classes and
    regulated functions.
   Immutable audit of model, data, policy, code and control versions involved in each decision.
   Research/simulation may be broader than executable scope; unsupported opportunities remain shadow-

26.1 Native Rust Event & Control Fabric
Algorik uses a native Rust messaging and journal subsystem as its internal asynchronous nervous system. It
replaces Google Pub/Sub and core Kafka dependencies so control semantics, replay, partitioning, backpressure,
regional independence and binary/runtime behavior can be designed around Algorik instead of around a
generic broker. The fabric is intentionally not the execution hot path: local market-event-to-order decisions and
direct peer Reflex Mesh coordination remain independent.

Protocol and semantics
       Transport: QUIC over UDP with mutual TLS for broker/client and cross-region mirror traffic; long-lived
        streams for durable records and QUIC datagrams only for explicitly lossy telemetry classes.
       Wire format: versioned Protobuf via prost for contracts and compatibility; BLAKE3/content hashes and CRC
        protection on records/segments. Hot in-process data structures may use Rust-native zero-copy layouts but
        never leak unversioned memory formats onto the network.
       Ordering: strict per-partition order. No false promise of a total global order. Every record carries source
        event time, receive time, region, partition, leader epoch, offset and hybrid logical clock metadata.
       Delivery: at-most-once allowed only for telemetry; at-least-once is default for durable streams; idempotent
        producer IDs/sequence numbers and deterministic event IDs provide effectively-once processing with
        idempotent sinks.
       Durability classes: Control uses three-zone quorum plus persistent metadata/WAL; financial outcomes use
        quorum replication and archival; market journals use high-throughput replicated segments; telemetry can
        be sampled/dropped under pressure.
       Backpressure: every stream has explicit byte/message quotas, lag limits and overload policy. Critical
        control/outcomes cannot be silently dropped; telemetry and low-value ambient signals can be sampled or
        shed.
       Cross-region behavior: selected streams mirror asynchronously over Google private networking. A regional
        cluster never blocks on remote acknowledgement, preventing a remote outage from stopping local execution
        or control consumption.

Hot-path contract
The Reflex Node publishes through a non-blocking local ring/journal writer running on a separate core. Order-
book processing, model inference, risk veto and venue send never wait on a broker acknowledgement. Model
and policy packages are stored as immutable signed artifacts in object/artifact storage; the fabric carries only

33. Final Definition of Algorik
Algorik is a globally distributed cognitive financial AGI target system. It continuously discovers and evaluates
world information; captures market reality tick by tick; maintains a federation of competing world and causal
models; reasons neurally, symbolically and counterfactually; runs proactive ambient models; and uses classical
and quantum computation as heterogeneous research and optimization tools. It manages assets, capital,
liquidity, collateral and risk; creates and trades opportunities across financial, event and physical markets;
coordinates regional reflex nodes for local and multi-leg execution; and maintains one authoritative ledger.
Beyond predicting futures, Algorik also models agency: it can represent a desired outcome, identify lawful
controllable levers, generate and simulate interventions, act through authorized tools, measure whether the
action actually changed the outcome, and learn a better causal action policy. Its intelligence may become
increasingly general, but every action that can move money, alter an external system or communicate publicly is
bounded by explicit identity, authority, truthfulness, conduct, feasibility, audit and rollback controls.
Its internal asynchronous nervous system is the Algorik Rust Event & Control Fabric: regionally independent,
replayable, backpressure-aware and selectively mirrored. The fabric carries control, journals and outcomes,
while the local Reflex hot path and direct Reflex Mesh remain independent for deterministic speed and failure
isolation.

    world models. Reason neurally and symbolically. Predict continuously. Specify desired outcomes. Discover
    controllable causal levers. Simulate interventions. Act only through authorized truthful channels and tools.
    Measure causal effect. Allocate intelligently. React locally. Coordinate globally. Account exactly. Learn from
    every actual and counterfactual outcome. Expand capability safely.
