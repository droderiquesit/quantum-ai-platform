# Master Blueprint v12.0 against v11.6: what is new or changed

Method. Both `.txt` files in `source/` were stripped of running headers, footers and page markers, split into sentences, and compared sentence by sentence. Everything not listed here matched v11.6 word for word apart from page-break shifts. The 853 raw `diff` lines reduce to the changes below. Pages are v12.0 PDF pages (the `=== page N ===` markers). Quotes are verbatim from `algorik-master-blueprint-v12.0.txt`.

Reading rules. Nothing here authorises anything (ADR 0099). "Conflict" means a clash with a standing decision. "C1-C8" is ADR 0099's register. "Domain" is the closest code in `requirements/*.json`, checked by grepping titles and statements. Where the match is weak it says so. ADRs I did not open (0005, 0006, 0007, 0083, 0089) are named only as far as the rules files describe them, and are marked "unread".

Scope of change: the delta is almost purely additive. v12.0 inserts 23 numbered subsections (§9.6-9.14, §11.1-11.5, §13.1-13.3, §14.1-14.3, §22.1, §24.9, §31.1), 7 contracts (§24.9), 10 completeness targets (§31.1), and rewrites the title block and §33. It deletes no section. Sections 3-8, 10, 12, 15-21, 23, 24.1-24.8, 25-30 and 32 are unchanged (their text matches v11.6).

## C. Unknowns the blueprint leaves undecided

1. **Where any of it runs.** §26 (pp40-41) and §27 (pp42-44) are unchanged. There is no placement row for the NOW Brain, forecast lattice, tournament, Forecast Market, Compute Router, Hedge Brain or Capital Kernel, and no GPU/TPU/QPU capacity row beyond the old training rows (p40-41).
2. **Build order.** §30 (p47-48) has no v12 phase. Phase 8 still reads "Financial AGI Autonomy". Where §9.6-9.14, §11.1-11.5, §13.1-13.3 and §14.1-14.3 land is not stated.
3. **Failure behaviour.** §28 (p45-46) is unchanged: no row for NOW Brain staleness, a calibration SLO breach, router failure, a poisoned tournament, or a Hedge Brain failure.
4. **No numbers.** The calibration SLO (p20), "thousands of models" (p19), "cheapest" observation and marginal-value thresholds (p20), and what "contract automatically" means (p28) all have no threshold. "Calibration is a production SLO" names no target.
5. **Authority of the new brains.** Who may veto or shrink exposure: Hedge Brain, Model-Risk Brain, Capital Kernel or the Risk Gate (pp27-28, p50 vs §14). Not stated.
6. **Synthetic capital.** Whether the Forecast Market's "synthetic reputation/capital" (p19) and "Internal synthetic capital/reputation" (p32) are ever denominated against or convertible to a real `CapitalGrant`. Not stated.
7. **Durable stores.** Nothing says whether NOW state, forecast lattice, branch trees and scoreboard are in the ledger, the event log or a new store (p18-20, p32).
8. **Name equivalences.** Capital Kernel vs Capital Survival Kernel (p26-27); Forecast vs ForecastState (p20, p38); Compute Intelligence Router vs Hybrid Compute Router (p24-25); "Attention Market" (p20) vs Attention Router (v11.6 §24.7).
9. **Solver Registry** (p25) is not among the §23.2 registries.
10. **Active-sensing probes.** "market microstructure probe" (p20) is not defined. It is not stated whether a probe is ever a live order. Treat it as `EXTERNAL_ACTION` until defined.
11. **Figures** on p21 and p26 are graphics and not in the text extraction. Their content is unreviewed.
